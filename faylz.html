Faylz Privacy Policy & Support

CODIFYS LLC · Effective November 27, 2026 · iOS app

Local storage and collection

Faylz stores folders, property details, checklists, imported files and scanned documents in private storage on your device. It does not require an account, send documents to a server, or contain advertising, analytics or tracking services. CODIFYS LLC does not collect usage data or documents through the app.

Camera, imports and text recognition

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document. Files are copied into the app after you select them through Apple's file picker. Category suggestions use on-device text recognition when supported by your operating system and language. Recognized text is not sent to CODIFYS LLC. You can review and change the category manually.

Sharing and backups

When you choose to share a PDF or export a property backup, your chosen destination app or storage provider receives the selected files. Its policies govern those copies. Property ZIP backups are not encrypted; keep them in a private, secure location.

The local archive is excluded from automatic device backups. Uninstalling Faylz deletes its local archive. Export documents and property backups you want to keep before uninstalling or changing devices.

Deletion

You can delete documents and folders within Faylz. Uninstalling the app removes local app data. Delete shared or exported copies from the destination where you saved them.

Support and contact

For help importing documents, organizing folders, sharing PDFs or restoring a property backup, email codifys.apps@gmail.com. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Please do not send sensitive identity documents in support emails.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Your documents are never automatically sent to us.
