الحفظ والمشاركة

الاشتراكات

تتم الاشتراكات عبر Google Play. يعالج Google معلومات الدفع وحساب Google وفق سياساته. يتلقى التطبيق حالة الشراء ومعرّف المنتج ورمز الشراء للتحقق من الوصول وتأكيد الشراء مع Google Play. لا يتلقى Codifys تفاصيل بطاقتك المصرفية. يمكنك إدارة الاشتراك أو إلغاؤه من Google Play؛ حذف التطبيق وحده لا يلغي الاشتراك.

الحسابات والخدمات

لا يتطلب التطبيق حساب Codifys ولا يضم إعلانات أو أدوات تحليلات. تستخدم خدمات Google Play الاتصال بالإنترنت لإدارة الاشتراكات. قد تعالج Google بيانات مرتبطة بخدماتها وفق سياسة خصوصية Google.

Privacy policy — Sawer w Bee

Codifys LLC · November 8, 2026

The app processes product photos, names, prices, descriptions, and WhatsApp numbers on your device to create sales cards. It does not upload those inputs to Codifys servers. Your camera app captures photos and the system picker provides images you select.

Shop branding is stored on your device. Photos and generated cards are cached locally. Delete the logo in the app or clear app data to remove local files. Android may remove temporary files automatically. When you share a card, your chosen recipient app receives read access to that image and handles it under its own policy. Captions go to the clipboard when you request copying.

Subscriptions use Google Play. Google handles payment and account information. The app receives purchase status, product IDs, and purchase tokens to check access and acknowledge purchases with Google Play. Codifys does not receive your bank-card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

No Codifys account is required. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools. Google Play services use an internet connection for billing and may process service data under Google's privacy policy.

This seller tool is not directed at children. Codifys does not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app. Changes to these practices will be reflected here. Contact info@codifys.com.
