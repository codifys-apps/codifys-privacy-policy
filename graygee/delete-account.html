Request GRAYGEE Account and Data Deletion

GRAYGEE (گريجي) is operated by Codifys. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data even if you no longer have the Android app installed.

How to make a request

  1. In GRAYGEE, open My Account (حسابي) and select Request deletion of my account and data (طلب حذف حسابي وبياناتي). Your email app will prepare a request.
  2. Or email codifys.apps@gmail.com with the subject GRAYGEE account and data deletion request. Include the email address used for your GRAYGEE account, and indicate whether you represent a travel agency.
  3. Codifys will review account ownership and confirm any verification steps before processing the request. Do not send your password, ID documents, or payment information in the initial email.

What deletion covers

The request covers your GRAYGEE Firebase Authentication account and associated personal account profile, inquiries, agency follows, trip interests, private questions and replies, content-rules receipt, and block list. Related reports are reviewed for deletion or lawful retention. If your account controls a travel agency, related agency details, contact records, promotions and trip listings must also be reviewed and cleaned up. The operator may need to contact you to clarify handling of published trips and associated traveler inquiries. If an agency stored your contact details, you can request correction or removal from that agency or email Codifys with the agency name and the contact information you want reviewed.

What may be retained

Data may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, security investigations, dispute resolution or operational backups. We will explain applicable exceptions during the request review. Deleting the app from your device alone does not delete your account or server-side data.

Questions

Contact codifys.apps@gmail.com. Read the GRAYGEE Privacy Policy.
