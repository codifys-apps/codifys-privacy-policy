GRAYGEE Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

GRAYGEE (گريجي) is an Android travel discovery and inquiry app operated by Codifys. This policy describes data used in the version providing traveler accounts, travel-agency registration, published trips and traveler inquiries. GRAYGEE does not currently accept in-app payments or confirm paid travel bookings.

Information you provide

  • Account: email address, authentication information managed by Firebase Authentication, and email-verification state.
  • Optional profile: display name and any profile contact details entered in the app.
  • Travel agencies: organization name, contact details (such as phone, WhatsApp, email and address), agency approval status, and trip listings including descriptions, itinerary, dates, advertised price and capacity.
  • Travel inquiries: your inquiry message, the relevant trip and agency, inquiry status and associated account identifiers.

Approved agency profiles and published trip descriptions can be shown to other users. Traveler inquiries are intended to be accessible to the submitting traveler and the relevant agency, not as public listings.

GRAYGEE stores whether you accepted its content rules, the accounts you choose to block, and any reports you submit. A report contains the reporter’s account ID, a reference to the reported item or account, the selected reason, the time and its review status. Block lists are private. Reports are available to authorized moderation staff.

How information is used

We use this information to sign users in, verify email addresses, review agency registrations, publish eligible agency trips, route traveler inquiries, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues and support requests relating to accounts and data. Travel agencies can see inquiries submitted to them, including the message and associated account identifier.

Firebase services and processing

GRAYGEE uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for authentication and app data. Google may process technical, security and diagnostic information needed to operate these services under its own terms and policies. Firestore database configuration for this app uses the Dammam region (me-central2); authentication or other service-related processing may occur in additional locations under Google's service arrangements.

The current Android app code does not implement advertising or in-app payment processing. If we add analytics, advertising, payments, or other services later, this policy and the Play Store disclosures must be reviewed and updated first.

Storage, security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections supported by Firebase. Firestore security rules restrict access to account data and private inquiries; however, no service can promise absolute security. We retain information while needed to provide the service, resolve support or security matters, and comply with applicable obligations. Data may remain in backups or records for a limited additional period where legally or operationally required.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion, use My Account → Request deletion of my account and data in the app, or see the web account deletion instructions. You may also contact codifys.apps@gmail.com. We may ask for proof of account ownership. A request is not the same as immediate deletion: linked agency trip listings and inquiries require appropriate review and cleanup to avoid leaving orphaned records. We will explain any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

Children and geographic scope

The app is intended for people using travel discovery and agency services. It is not specifically designed for children. Do not send highly sensitive personal data (such as payment card numbers or passport details) in inquiry messages.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, email codifys.apps@gmail.com. We may update this policy as the app changes; the effective date above identifies this version.
