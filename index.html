Baghdad Resale Calculator

Privacy Policy | Codifys LLC

Effective date: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Codifys LLC ("we", "our", or "us") develops Baghdad Resale Calculator, an Android application designed to help users calculate product costs, resale prices, profits, and profit margins.

This policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

2. Information You Enter

The application allows users to enter product quantities, purchase prices, shipping costs, currency exchange rates, and selling prices. These values are used to perform calculations within the application.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The application is designed to perform calculations locally on your device. Codifys LLC does not receive or collect the calculation values entered into the application, and does not sell or share those values with third parties.

4. Data Storage

The application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information. Calculation inputs are processed on the device. The application does not upload calculation data to Codifys LLC servers.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The calculator is designed to operate without an internet connection. The application does not intentionally integrate advertising or analytics services.

Distribution through Google Play may involve information processed independently by Google under Google's own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is intended as a general-purpose business calculation tool and is not specifically directed toward children. Codifys LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

7. Security

The application is designed to minimize data collection by processing calculations locally. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into calculation fields.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when application features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
